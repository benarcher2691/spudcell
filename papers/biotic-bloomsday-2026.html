Welcome

For the first time, a simple cell can be built from defined chemical components and made to grow, divide, and be selected across generations. The open question is no longer whether synthetic cells are possible. It is: what will it take to make the engineering of cellular systems routine, and who holds the foundations of this platform.

Biotic is a public-benefit research organisation established to build foundations of global, democratized synthetic cell engineering in the open.

On Bloomsday, we work with researchers who will build the next generation of bioengineering, the ethicists who will guide it, and the policymakers who will govern it, in one room.

Biotic’s approach

Foundational research

Work on technologies the whole field depends on, unlocking possibilities no single laboratory can reach alone.

Biotic’s obligation

Transparency and rigour

Methods, data and materials are published and shared, so that results can be checked, repeated and built upon.

Biotic’s purpose

Benefit held in common

Capability that reaches all people and the planet, rather than accruing to whoever reaches it first.
